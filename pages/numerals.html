Ascending Place-Value Order, Explicit Additive Grouping, and Unambiguous Multiplication

Scholarly proposal · 22 October 2026
Applicable by convention to Early Qenya, Quenya, Sindarin, Neo-Quenya, and Neo-Sindarin

Abstract. This document proposes a unified, decimal numeral syntax for Tolkien's Elvish languages, adopting ascending place-value order, coefficient-before-denomination multiplication, and obligatory conjunctions between independent positional groups. The system is intended to resolve structural ambiguities while retaining selected features of Tolkien's attested Early Qenya numerals. It is a proposed Neo-Eldarin convention, not a claim that Tolkien established such a universal system.

1. Fundamental Recommendation

The recommended model is a modified Malagasy-style numeration system, with ascending place-value order both within and between numerical groups. Multipliers precede the denominations they quantify. Independent additive groups must be separated by an explicit conjunction.

The six governing principles are:

  1. Arrange positional groups in ascending magnitude.
  2. Arrange units, tens, and hundreds in ascending order within each group.
  3. Place a complete coefficient before its denomination.
  4. Require a spoken conjunction between independent groups.
  5. Restrict multiplicative scope through explicit grammar.
  6. Maintain one canonical representation per numerical value.

This approach preserves more of the relevant Early Qenya ordering patterns than an English-style descending paradigm, while avoiding ambiguities arising from unrestricted numerical juxtaposition.

2. Historical Foundations

The principal evidence comes from the Early Qenya Grammar, published in Parma Eldalamberon 14. The following expressions are reported as Tolkien's own examples.

These examples demonstrate the coexistence of ascending additive order and coefficient-before-denomination multiplication. They do not establish a universal rule for complex multipliers of thousands or millions.

3. Structural Ambiguity

Consider the Neo-Sindarin numerical vocabulary: eneg (six), nelphaen (thirty), and meneg (thousand).

Without explicit grouping, a hypothetical sequence eneg nelphaen meneg of these elements may permit competing arithmetical interpretations:

InterpretationArithmeticValue
Grouped multiplier (6 + 30) × 1,00036,000
Separate components 6 + (30 × 1,000)30,006
Additive elements 6 + 30 + 1,0001,036

The problem is not ascending order itself, but the absence of a rule specifying where a multiplier begins and ends and where addition takes place.

4. Canonical Decimal Grouping

Rule I — Divide numbers into positional groups

Every number is represented as a sum of groups, each containing at most three decimal digits:

N = g0 + 10³g1 + 10⁶g2 + …
0 ≤ gi ≤ 999

Zero-valued groups are omitted. Nonzero groups are spoken in ascending order, beginning with the units and proceeding through thousands, millions, and successively higher denominations.

Rule II — Internal ascending order

Within a coefficient of 1–999, components occur in the sequence:

Units → Tens → Hundreds

The hundreds component is internally multiplicative: a single digit followed by the hundred denomination. The coefficient of a hundred must be explicit when a lower-valued component precedes it.

ValueStructural expression
36six thirty
100hundred
106six one-hundred
120twenty one-hundred
236six thirty two-hundred
456six fifty four-hundred

These English expressions are mathematical templates, not translations. Hyphens illustrate component grouping, not prescribed Elvish orthography.

For instance, the proposed canonical construction of 120 differs from historical Early Qenya yukainen tuksa. The historical form is retained as an attestation, but excluded from the new standardised grammar.

Rule III — Multiplier before denomination

A complete coefficient precedes its higher denomination and is multiplied by that denomination.

[6 + 30] × 1,000 = 36,000
[6 + 30 + 200] × 1,000 = 236,000

The multiplier cannot include an earlier independent positional group across an additive conjunction. This is an editorial extension beyond Tolkien's attested examples.

Rule IV — Obligatory additive conjunction

Separate nonzero positional groups must be linked by an overt conjunction equivalent to "and". It is prohibited inside an individual coefficient.

ValueCanonical structural expression
1,036 six thirty AND thousand
30,006 six AND thirty thousand
36,000 six thirty thousand
36,036 six thirty AND six thirty thousand
123,456 six fifty four-hundred AND three twenty one-hundred thousand

The conjunction has additive force only. It cannot be interpreted as part of a multiplicative coefficient.

Rule V — Ascending denomination order

Each higher denomination may occur at most once, and denominations must appear in strictly ascending order across groups.

For example, 1,001,036 has the structural expression:

six thirty AND thousand AND million

Descending arrangements are disallowed in the canonical register.

Rule VI — Restricted multiplication

  • A hundred has a coefficient between 1 and 9.
  • Thousands and higher denominations have coefficients between 1 and 999.
  • A coefficient of one may be omitted before a thousand or a higher denomination.
  • Unrestricted nesting of scale words is prohibited.
  • Each numerical value has one canonical positional representation.

For example, 3,600 is expressed conceptually as 600 + (3 × 1,000), not as 36 × 100.

5. Schematic Neo-Sindarin Examples

The following expressions illustrate grouping with the lexical elements eneg, nelphaen, and meneg. They are not represented as morphologically verified Sindarin sentences. In particular, mutation and numeral–noun inflection remain to be established independently.

Here a is the attested Sindarin conjunction "and", assigned an obligatory group-boundary function by the proposed standard. These complete expressions are reconstructed syntactic illustrations, not Tolkien attestations.

6. Formal Grammar

NUMBER :=
    BLOCK (AND BLOCK)*

BLOCK :=
    SMALL_GROUP
    | SCALE
    | SMALL_GROUP SCALE

SMALL_GROUP :=
    BELOW_HUNDRED
    | HUNDRED
    | HUNDREDS_COMPONENT
    | BELOW_HUNDRED HUNDREDS_COMPONENT

BELOW_HUNDRED :=
    NUMBER_1_TO_19
    | TENS
    | UNIT TENS

HUNDREDS_COMPONENT :=
    DIGIT HUNDRED

SCALE :=
    THOUSAND
    | MILLION
    | BILLION
    | ...

Constraints:
1. UNIT and DIGIT have values 1–9.
2. TENS has values 20–90.
3. SMALL_GROUP has value 1–999.
4. BLOCKS occur in ascending scale order.
5. Each scale appears no more than once.
6. AND separates complete positional blocks.
7. HUNDRED alone is permitted only when no lower component precedes it.
8. A SCALE takes only a coefficient 1–999.

The conjunction first divides the expression into independent blocks. Each block is evaluated internally, and its denomination determines its place-value multiplier. The resulting block values are added.

Under these restrictions, the proposed grammar assigns a single numerical interpretation to each well-formed canonical expression.

8. Contradictions and Historical Limitations

PE21:77; VT49:45 Later numeral–noun syntax may conflict with the selected Early Qenya-style ordering.
Early meanings of tuksa Decimal and duodecimal historical values must not be mixed without an explicit base.
VT42; VT47 Decimal and duodecimal practices require separate evaluation.
WJ:415, Menegroth The independent exact cardinal sense of Sindarin meneg needs qualification.

These conflicts are not erased by the proposal. They demonstrate that the standard is a selective editorial system, rather than a reconstruction of an otherwise complete historical Tolkienian grammar.

9. Language-Wide Application

The same mathematical grammar may be adopted conventionally across Early Qenya, Quenya, Sindarin, Neo-Quenya and Neo-Sindarin. However, the actual lexical and morphological expressions must be developed separately for each language variety.

This includes:

  • Exact denomination vocabulary.
  • Numeral inflection and noun agreement.
  • Consonant mutations and sandhi.
  • The correct form of the additive conjunction.
  • Language-specific treatment of teens and tens.
  • Potential distinct duodecimal vocabulary.

A form that is historically Early Qenya should not automatically be treated as later Quenya. Similarly, a Neo-Sindarin formation should never be represented as a Tolkien-attested word.

10. Attestation Categories

Every finished lexical or grammatical form should receive one of three labels:

  1. Attested: directly recorded by Tolkien in an identified published source.
  2. Plausibly derived: constructed using identified attested elements and a justified rule; explicitly labeled Neo-.
  3. Unattested and rejected: not sufficiently supported for use as a verified translation.

The universal grouping rules proposed here belong to the second category at the level of grammatical design. Individual completed word forms may require further verification.

11. Final Recommendation

The recommended canonical numeral system is ascending, decimal, explicitly grouped, and coefficient-first.

It retains selected Early Qenya compositional principles while introducing compulsory additive boundaries, controlled multiplication, and a restriction against repeated denominations.

For the original example, the system distinguishes the three values structurally:

ValueArithmetic
36,000 (6 + 30) × 1,000
30,006 6 + (30 × 1,000)
1,036 (6 + 30) + 1,000

This distinction is explicit in both spoken and written form, provided that the standardized conjunction and morphology are implemented consistently.

The historical evidence supplies the constituent numeral principles; the new standard supplies the previously unspecified boundaries of arithmetic scope.
