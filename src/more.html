External resources

[The Sindarin Supply Wordbook](https://sindarin.dictionary.elvish.nz) - A dictionary with neologisms and commentary, trying to cover the most essential vocabulary.

[Quettamorphosis](https://quettamorphosis.elvish.nz/) - A tool for playing with the phonetic changes of Sindarin and Quenya (the latter is still being developed.)

[Bardh, Sindarin Crash Course by Rínor](https://sindarincrashcourse.neocities.org/) - An excellent website with a more conservative (and thus more accepted) approach to Neo-Sindarin, with tools to help with verb conjugation, pluralisation of nouns and adjectives, stress analiser and many more useful stuff.

[Tecendil - The Tengwar Transcriber](https://www.tecendil.com) - A great tool to transcribe Eldarin text into tengwar.

[Glǽmscrafu - Tolkien's linguistic cellar](https://glaemscrafu.jrrvf.com/) - A website dedicated to all Tolkien languages, with examples, sound files, an alphabet transcriber, and references.

[A Sindarin and Noldorin dictionary](https://www.jrrvf.com/hisweloke/sindar/index.html) - A Sindarin-French dictionary focusing on attested words and their sources.

[Eldamo - An Elvish Lexicon](https://eldamo.org/) - Paul Strack's notorious website, the most thorough lexicon of Eldarin languages available.

[Ardalambion](https://www.ardalambion.org/) - The oldest and most comprehensive website on Tolkien languages. Its Quenya and Sindarin sections are a bit outdated, but otherwise, still a great source of information all over.

[RealElvish Academy](https://academy.realelvish.net/) - A source of linguists analyses, translations and latest news about the Eldarin languages. This website contains articles by reputable linguists and people who dedicate quite a lot of time to uncover the secrets of Tolkien's legendarium.
